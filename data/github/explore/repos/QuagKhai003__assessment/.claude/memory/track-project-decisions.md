During the Gradion assessment work, the user wants me to note/track their decisions as we make them throughout the conversation — but only decisions related to the project, not everything.

**Why:** Assessment requires a `DECISIONS.md` capturing real trade-offs (§2.1); tracking decisions live avoids back-filling, which scores badly.

**How to apply:** Record a decision only AFTER the user explicitly confirms it — never mid-discussion or from my own proposal the user hasn't accepted (the user corrected me for recording storage before they had decided). Once confirmed, record it (DECISIONS.md entry). Do not log trivia or non-project chatter.

Always capture the REASONING behind each decision, both sides: when the user pushes back or makes their own call, record their reasoning; when the user accepts my proposal, record my reasoning too. Every entry should say why, not just what (matches §2.1: who proposed, who pushed back, where it landed, the cost).
